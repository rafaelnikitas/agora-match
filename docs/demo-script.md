## Narration

### One match. Three perspectives.

Agora means a public gathering place. Agora Match brings fans, analysts, and coaches together around one game, with different perspectives.

### Follow the action at your own pace

Choose either fictional fixture. Follow the ball, change replay speed, or jump straight to a key moment.

### Ask why this moment matters

As a fan, ask why a corner matters. Agora combines the current event with both teams' recent history, then explains the relevant counts in plain language.

### Open the facts. See the AI work.

Open the evidence and the actual AI trace. A match analyst selects facts, a narrator explains them, and a reviewer checks the draft. Microsoft Foundry powers all three stages.

### Turn a question into a visual investigation

For an analyst, a question becomes a visual investigation. Find fullback versus winger encounters, select a case, and inspect its outcome alongside the sampled positions.

### Your evidence follows the match clock

Rewind to twenty minutes. The available cases shrink to one, because later evidence is excluded from that moment.

### See the match through one player

Follow one player through a movement heatmap, position-specific statistics, a transparent demo rating, and comparisons with recent synthetic history.

### Know what to review next

The coach gets a priority board and an assistant. Inspect a widening gap or changing running output, check the evidence, and prepare a discussion. These are review cues, not fatigue diagnoses.

### Read the commentary. Or listen.

Generate match commentary from the available evidence. The Speak control can read it aloud through the browser.

### Carry the insight into a broadcast

For a studio workflow, preview a timed insight card and export its evidence and timing as JSON.

### Live on Azure. Evidence you can inspect.

This app runs on Azure Container Apps, with keyless access to Foundry. Twenty-one live acceptance checks passed; explanation quality still needs human review.

### The story behind the score

Two synthetic games. Three perspectives. Open Agora Match, choose your role, and ask the match.
